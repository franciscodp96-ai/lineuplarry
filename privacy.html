Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Lineup Larry is a private tool run by a single person (the channel owner) on their own computer. It uses the YouTube Data API, the Instagram Graph API, the Twitch API and the Telegram Bot API only to find public gameplay clips and to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube and Instagram accounts.

What data we access

  • Owner's accounts: OAuth tokens for the owner's YouTube channel and Instagram account, used only to upload, update and delete the channel's own videos. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and never shared.
  • Public data: public clip metadata (title, view count, creator name, URL) from Twitch and YouTube, used to choose and credit clips.

What we do not do

  • We do not offer sign-in to other users and do not collect personal data from viewers.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.

Lineup Larry's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time at Google account permissions; local tokens are deleted with the tool. Creators who want a clip removed can write to the address below and it will be deleted.
